How these numbers work

Delivered orders with valid promised and actual dates. Delivered on or before promise.
Delivery time
Average elapsed time from dispatch to actual delivery.
Cancellation
Cancelled orders divided by all placed orders in the filtered view.
Small samples
Segments below 10 eligible deliveries are flagged; treat their rates as directional.
